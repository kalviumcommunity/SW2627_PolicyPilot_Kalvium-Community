# PolicyPilot Customer & Employee Data Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction and Scope
PolicyPilot ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and confidential information of our customers, merchant partners, and employees. This Privacy Policy sets forth our practices regarding the collection, storage, processing, transfer, and protection of personal data in accordance with global data protection standards including GDPR, CCPA, and applicable local data privacy regulations. This policy applies to all interactions with the PolicyPilot platform, enterprise services, customer support portals, and mobile applications.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect personal information necessary to deliver and improve our services, facilitate e-commerce operations, and fulfill statutory obligations:
- Personal Identifiers: Full legal name, business contact email, phone number, physical billing address, shipping address, and unique user account credentials.
- Transaction and Payment Records: Payment method identifiers, transaction histories, invoice logs, order fulfillment details, and refund/chargeback records.
- Technical and Telemetry Data: IP address, device fingerprints, operating system specifications, browser attributes, session duration, clickstream paths, and interaction telemetry.
- Commercial and Support Records: Customer service ticket correspondence, live chat transcripts, returns documentation, and dispute resolution evidence.

## 3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Processing
We process personal data only under explicit legal grounds:
- Contractual Necessity: Processing required to authenticate accounts, process transactions, dispatch orders, deliver customer support, and execute seller merchant agreements.
- Legitimate Business Interests: Monitoring platform health, fraud detection and risk prevention, quality assurance, system performance optimization, and product analytics.
- Legal and Regulatory Compliance: Complying with tax reporting requirements, anti-money laundering (AML) mandates, audit disclosures, and lawful judicial requests.
- Explicit User Consent: Delivering opt-in promotional updates, personalized policy recommendations, and elective survey participation.

## 4. Cookies and Automated Tracking Technologies
PolicyPilot utilizes essential, functional, and analytical cookies to maintain user sessions and secure platform access:
- Essential Session Cookies: Required for core authentication, CSRF security tokens, shopping cart persistence, and secure checkout navigation.
- Analytical and Performance Cookies: Gather aggregated and anonymized usage metrics to diagnose system latency, improve navigation flows, and assess feature adoption.
- Cookie Governance: Users may customize or withdraw non-essential cookie permissions at any time through the Privacy Preference Center in account settings.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing and Data Transfers
PolicyPilot does not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties. We share personal information strictly under contractual confidentiality agreements with:
- Payment Processors and Financial Gateways: To securely tokenize credit cards, process payments, and disburse merchant payouts.
- Logistics and Delivery Couriers: To verify physical addresses and complete package transport and delivery.
- Cloud Infrastructure and Security Providers: For encrypted data hosting, automated database backups, DDoS mitigation, and endpoint threat monitoring.
- Regulatory and Law Enforcement Authorities: Solely when formally compelled by valid subpoena, court order, or applicable statutory law.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Schedule
We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the business purposes for which it was gathered:
- Active Account Profiles: Retained for the duration of the active customer relationship plus 2 years following account deactivation.
- Financial and Transaction Records: Retained for a mandatory period of 7 years in accordance with federal tax and financial auditing standards.
- Telemetry and Access Logs: Rotated and permanently purged after 180 days.
- Deletion Requests: Upon verified deletion requests under statutory privacy laws, non-regulated records are purged within 30 calendar days.

## 7. Technical and Organizational Security Safeguards
We deploy robust multi-layered security controls to protect information assets against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or breach:
- Encryption Standards: All data in transit is protected using TLS 1.3 encryption, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 standards.
- Identity and Access Management: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) enforced with mandatory multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege principles.
- Continuous Security Auditing: Periodic third-party vulnerability assessments, penetration testing, and automated vulnerability scanning across all codebase repositories.
- Incident Response Protocol: Documented breach notification procedures with 72-hour reporting commitments to relevant supervisory authorities.

## 8. User Rights and Choices
Depending on your jurisdiction, you have enforceable privacy rights regarding your personal information:
- Right to Access and Data Portability: Request an export of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable JSON or CSV format.
- Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated personal information via your account profile or customer support.
- Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request permanent deletion of your personal data subject to legal retention obligations.
- Right to Restrict or Object to Processing: Restrict certain processing activities or opt out of automated profiling and direct marketing communications.
- Exercising Rights: Submit privacy requests through the Data Subject Request Portal or by emailing privacy@policypilot.internal. Requests are acknowledged within 48 hours and fulfilled within 30 days.

## 9. Updates to This Policy
PolicyPilot may periodically revise this Privacy Policy to reflect regulatory amendments, new platform features, or evolved data handling practices. When material modifications occur, we will notify registered users via in-app banner notifications or email 14 days prior to implementation. The "Effective Date" at the top of this document indicates the latest revision.
