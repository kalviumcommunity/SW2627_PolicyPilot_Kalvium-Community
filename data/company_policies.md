# PolicyPilot Official Company Guidelines

## Section 1: Annual Leave and Time Off Policy
All full-time employees are eligible for 20 paid annual leave days per calendar year. Paid time off must be requested at least 5 business days in advance via the HR Employee Portal and approved by the department manager. Sick leave is granted up to 10 days per year with medical certificate documentation for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days.

## Section 2: Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees traveling for official company business are entitled to reimbursement for transport, lodging, and daily meal allowances. Expense claims must be submitted with itemized original receipts within 14 days following the completion of travel. Meal expenses are capped at $60 per day.

## Section 3: IT Hardware and Remote Work Allowance Policy
Full-time employees working remotely are provided a standard hardware allocation consisting of a company-issued laptop, secondary monitor, and ergonomic peripheral kit. Replacement or upgrade of hardware equipment occurs every 3 years. Equipment claims must be logged through the IT Helpdesk ticket system.

## Section 4: Working Hours and Flexible Workplace Guidelines
Standard operating hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday, with mandatory core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM. Employees may request flexible start/end times with approval from their team supervisor.
